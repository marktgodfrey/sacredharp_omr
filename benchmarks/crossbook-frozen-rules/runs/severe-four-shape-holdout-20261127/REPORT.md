Before inference, the lock explicitly disclosed a selection deviation: exact globally first-three eligible-head ordering could not be certified from page screening; the cohort uses earliest enlarged/adjudicated candidates. This weakens strict preregistration. Severity is approximate visual judgment; disagreements stay in five ambiguous cases. Some controls and moderate labels have only main review. No labels/crops changed after inference and no reference XML/confidence/PD/F1 score guided selection.

This is an enriched convenience sample, with paired layouts rather than independent replications. Shared repertoire/possible printing ancestry and unknown training provenance limit independence. Source ink loss may originate in printing, scanning or layered-PDF compression; physical cause is unproven. These observed cases must not be reused as a fresh holdout for revised rules.
