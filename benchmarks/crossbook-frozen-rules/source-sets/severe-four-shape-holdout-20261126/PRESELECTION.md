## Search and stopping rule

Primary pools: (A) 1850 Auburn/VEMP Sacred Harp, printed pages 67-366
(PDF ordinals provisionally printed+3, verify pagination), excluding previously
viewed printed27-66; (B) local White & White New Sacred Harp 1884, all music pages
if its title/rudiments confirm four-shape notation. Inspect front matter only
to establish notation and provenance first. A non-four-shape source is excluded
by notation, not performance. Source B is a supplied local file; edition/date
will be caveated unless visible source pages support the filename.

Screen the full finite pool in ascending page order within each book, independent
of detections, verifier evidence, prior failure IDs, reference XML, or scores.
Partitioning source review among assistants is permitted; each must log pages
and candidates, avoid outputs, and not silently redefine damage. Collect the
first three severe candidates per shape per book, plus first three moderate
candidates per shape if severe coverage is absent. Finish source review and
lock the selected cases before any inference. Cap inference selection at the
first three severe candidates per shape per book; moderate candidates are a
separate labelled coverage cohort, not substitutes for severe success. Retain
source identity/severity disagreements as ambiguous secondary cases.

If either primary pool does not establish severe coverage in all four shapes,
select one additional publicly accessible historical four-shape scan by
catalogued poor/worn condition or visible physical-page damage, before using
predictions; document the source and a finite ordered page search in a dated
addendum before inspecting that scan's music. Do not adapt selection after
inference. Stop after the declared source pools and addendum pool even if some
shape/severity cells remain empty; report missing cells explicitly.

## Source-only labels

Shapes: triangle (fa), oval (sol), square (la), diamond (mi). A head identity
must be supported by surviving source contours, attached stem and staff context;
use adjacent undamaged examples of the same printed glyph where helpful.
Severe: approximately at least one quarter of an outline rim absent, at least
one third of expected solid head body absent, or multiple disconnected head
fragments with missing connecting contour. This is a visual operational label,
not a measured reconstruction. Moderate: a smaller definite rim/body break.
Faint but continuous ink, normal hollow counters, ordinary stem/staff overlaps,
paper damage outside music, and normal diamond/triangle corners do not qualify.
Classify uncertain shape, identity or damage severity explicitly as ambiguous;
do not force an all-shape claim. No whole head may be reconstructed from a
reference edition. No artificial erosion or copy/paste damage.

Include the complete tune containing each selected object and every complete
neighbour tune sharing the selected page. Continued tunes may be assembled from
adjacent native pages with documented translation/gaps only, or retained as
explicitly partial source-safety inputs if complete assembly is impractical.
Do not drop a selected difficult object because of layout. Mark exact source
head boxes, shape, fill, severity and uncertainty before inference. Include
first two intact heads of each shape found on each selected page as controls.
Use exact native-pixel crops; no enhancement/threshold/warping/rescaling.

## Frozen evaluation

Lock selection, crops, source bytes, protocol and engine mapping by SHA256 before
CPU_ONLY baseline capture. Run both layouts and both frozen policies on every
selected crop. No reference scoring is required. Separate source-presence,
front-end candidate coverage, serialized note survival and actual revised
abstention/rescue effects. A missing/misclassified/unselected head is never a
protection success. Check all removals/abstentions/changed output events, timing
invariant failures and lost baseline source IDs. Keep all failures in counts.
Paired layouts are not independent heads. Retention does not certify pitch,
voice or duration; this enriched sample is not book-wide accuracy.

No tuning or source/label/crop changes after inference. Preserve negative results
and unexercised mechanisms. These cases become observed material afterward.
Shared repertoire/plates and unknown model training provenance limit independence.
