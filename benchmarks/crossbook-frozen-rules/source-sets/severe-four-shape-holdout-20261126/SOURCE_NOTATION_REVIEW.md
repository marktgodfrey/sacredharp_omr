# Notation eligibility review

The local White & White New Sacred Harp (1884) PDF was excluded before any
music inference. Its copyright1884 cover and preface identify the work, and
PDF page3 explicitly says the New Sacred Harp uses the seven character system.
It therefore cannot supply a four-shape holdout. Only front matter was viewed.
No prediction or score informed this exclusion. Native front matter is saved
under pages/1884-pdf-001.jpg and 1884-pdf-003.jpg.

The 1850 Auburn copy has four-shape notation, already established in the earlier
source-only review. Printed27-66 are observed and excluded from the fresh pool.
The fresh pool is provisionally PDF70-369, printed67-366; readers must verify
printed page labels and record missing/duplicate pages rather than assume.
